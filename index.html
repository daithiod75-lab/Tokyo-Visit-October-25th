Comprehensive Day Trip Schedule (October 25, 2026)

06:30 - 09:30

Hitachi Station Departure to Tokyo Station

Depart early morning via JR Highway Bus or Express Train from Hitachi Station. Arrive at Tokyo Station (Nihonbashi / Yaesu Exit).

09:30 - 10:30

Tokyo Station Photo Op & Transit to Asakusa

Group photo session in front of the historic Tokyo Station Marunouchi building. Transfer via Ginza Line to Asakusa Station.

10:30 - 11:30

Asakusa Exploration & Senso-ji Temple

A 1-hour structured walking tour of Kaminarimon Gate, Nakamise-dori shopping street, and Senso-ji Temple with chaperones.

11:30 - 14:00

Tokyo Skytree Visit & Lunch (Observation Deck Tour)

Fast transit to Tokyo Skytree. Ascend to the Tembo Deck / Galleria for 10 participants. Enjoy lunch and panoramic Tokyo views at Solamachi.

14:00 - 16:00

Shinjuku Scramble Crossing & City Experience

Travel via train to Shinjuku. Experience iconic Tokyo streetscapes, group safety briefing around major pedestrian crossings, and brief break.

16:00 - 20:30

Tokyo Disney Resort (After 5PM Passport Entry)

Arrive at Tokyo Disney Resort before 17:00. Enter with After 5 Passport for evening attractions, dinner, and the spectacular evening parade.

20:30 - 23:30

Return Journey to Hitachi Station (Bus or Limited Express)

Board late-evening highway bus (e.g., 20:30 departure from Tokyo Station/Disney area) or Limited Express train back to Hitachi Station.

Leg 1 Hitachi ➔ Tokyo Station

Morning Highway Bus / Limited Express

Direct JR Highway Bus from Hitachi Station to Tokyo Station (Nihonbashi Exit), or Limited Express Train (Joban Line). Takes ~2 hours. Cost approx. ¥3,000 per person.

Duration: ~1 hr 45 min - 2 hrs
Cost (11 pax): ¥33,000 (approx)
Leg 2 Tokyo Station ➔ Asakusa

Tokyo Metro Ginza Line

Walk to Ginza Line platform at Tokyo/Nihonbashi, take direct subway to Asakusa Station (G19). Fast, cheap, and convenient for groups.

Duration: 15 minutes
Cost (11 pax): ¥2,200 (¥200 / person)
Leg 3 Asakusa ➔ Tokyo Skytree

Tobu Skytree Line / Walk across Sumida River

Fastest route: Tobu Skytree Line from Asakusa to Tokyo Skytree Station (1 stop, 3 mins) or a scenic 15-minute group walk across the Sumida River pedestrian bridge.

Duration: 3 mins train / 15 mins walk
Cost (11 pax): ¥1,760 train (¥160 / person) or Free (Walk)
Leg 4 Skytree ➔ Shinjuku ➔ Disney Resort

Train to Shinjuku, then JR Keiyo Line to Maihama

Subway/train transit to Shinjuku for the afternoon session, then group transfer via JR lines to Tokyo Station and direct to Maihama Station (Tokyo Disney Resort).

Duration: ~75 mins total transit
Cost (11 pax): Approx. ¥6,050 total

Return Transport Options: Tokyo Disney Resort / Tokyo to Hitachi Station

For returning to Hitachi after the evening events, two structured and reliable return options are available: direct highway bus schedules (such as 18:15 and 20:30 departures) or JR Limited Express (特急) trains from Tokyo/Ueno.

Option A: Direct Highway Bus (e.g., 20:30 Departure)

  • Route: Tokyo Station / Expressway bus connections from Tokyo Disney Resort area to Hitachi Station.
  • Departure Time: Evening departures around 18:15 or 20:30.
  • Duration: Approx. 2 hours 30 minutes.
  • Cost per person: Approx. ¥4,000 (Total for 11: ¥44,000).

Option B: JR Limited Express (特急) Train Breakdown

  • Route: Maihama to Tokyo/Ueno via JR Keiyo & Yamanote/Keihin-Tohoku Line, then Joban Line Limited Express (Hitachi/Tokiwa) to Hitachi Station.
  • Schedule: Frequent evening departures from Ueno/Tokyo (e.g., 20:00, 21:00, 22:00).
  • Duration: ~85 minutes from Ueno to Hitachi.
  • Cost per person: Unreserved/Reserved Limited Express fare approx. ¥4,200 - ¥4,800 per person.

Proposal Notes for Australian Guests and Hosts

Chaperone & Student Ratio

With 3 experienced chaperones supervising 8 high school students (a 1:2.6 ratio), student safety and accountability are maintained throughout all transit junctions (Tokyo Station, Asakusa, Shinjuku, and Disney Resort).

Time Management & Contingency

All scheduled stops have buffer times. The After 5PM passport ensures Australian guests and hosts enjoy the peak evening atmosphere and parade at Disney Resort without excessive fatigue before the late-evening return to Hitachi.

Trip Value & Experience Highlights

A good value for money experience that mixes ancient Tokyo with modern Tokyo and appeals to both young and not so young alike.
