Comprehensive Day Trip Schedule (October 25, 2026)

Structured timeline covering morning transit, cultural sightseeing, iconic Tokyo landmarks, evening dinner/shopping, and Limited Express train return.

06:50 - 09:31

Hitachi Station Departure to Tokyo Station

Depart early morning via JR Highway Bus or Express Train from Hitachi Station (06:50). Arrive at Tokyo Station Nihonbashi Exit at 09:31.

09:31 - 09:55

Tokyo Station Photo Op & Transit to Asakusa

Group photo session in front of the historic Tokyo Station Marunouchi building upon arrival. Transfer via Ginza Line to Asakusa Station.

10:00 - 11:45

Asakusa Exploration & Senso-ji Temple

A structured walking tour of Kaminarimon Gate, Nakamise-dori shopping street, and Senso-ji Temple with chaperones.

12:00 - 14:00

Tokyo Skytree Visit & Lunch (Observation Deck Tour)

Fast transit to Tokyo Skytree. Ascend to the Tembo Deck / Galleria for participants. Enjoy lunch and panoramic Tokyo views at Solamachi.

14:30 - 16:30

Shinjuku Scramble Crossing & City Experience

Travel via train to Shinjuku. Experience iconic Tokyo streetscapes, group safety briefing around major pedestrian crossings, and brief break.

17:00 - 18:45

Tokyo / Ueno Station Area Souvenir Shopping

Return to Tokyo or Ueno Station area for final souvenir shopping and train boarding preparation.

19:00 / 21:45

Limited Express Train Return to Hitachi Station

Board a direct JR Joban Line Limited Express train (such as Tokiwa or Hitachi) from Tokyo/Shinjuku back to Hitachi Station.

Leg 1 Hitachi ➔ Tokyo Station

Morning Highway Bus / Limited Express

Direct JR Highway Bus from Hitachi Station to Tokyo Station (Nihonbashi Exit), or Limited Express Train (Joban Line). Takes ~2 hours. Cost approx. ¥3,000 per person.

Duration: ~1 hr 45 min - 2 hrs
Cost (11 pax): ¥33,000 (approx)
Leg 2 Tokyo Station ➔ Asakusa

Tokyo Metro Ginza Line

Walk to Ginza Line platform at Tokyo/Nihonbashi, take direct subway to Asakusa Station (G19). Fast, cheap, and convenient for groups.

Duration: 15 minutes
Cost (11 pax): ¥2,200 (¥200 / person)
Leg 3 Asakusa ➔ Tokyo Skytree

Tobu Skytree Line / Walk across Sumida River

Fastest route: Tobu Skytree Line from Asakusa to Tokyo Skytree Station (1 stop, 3 mins) or a scenic 15-minute group walk across the Sumida River pedestrian bridge.

Duration: 3 mins train / 15 mins walk
Cost (11 pax): ¥1,760 train (¥160 / person) or Free (Walk)
Leg 4 Skytree ➔ Shinjuku ➔ Tokyo Station

Train to Shinjuku, then Return to Tokyo Station

Subway/train transit to Shinjuku for the afternoon session, then group transfer via JR lines back to Tokyo Station for evening dinner and shopping.

Duration: ~60 mins total transit
Cost (11 pax): Approx. ¥3,500 total

Return Transport Options: Tokyo / Shinjuku to Hitachi Station (Limited Express)

For returning to Hitachi after the evening schedule, comfortable and direct JR Joban Line Limited Express (特急) trains from Tokyo or Ueno stations are utilized (such as the 21:45 service).

Proposal Notes for Australian Guests and Hosts

Chaperone & Student Ratio

With 3 experienced chaperones supervising 8 high school students (a 1:2.6 ratio), student safety and accountability are maintained throughout all transit junctions (Tokyo Station, Asakusa, Skytree, and Shinjuku).

Time Management & Contingency

All scheduled stops have buffer times. The structured itinerary ensures Australian guests and hosts enjoy cultural sights and modern landmarks without excessive fatigue before the return to Hitachi.

Trip Value & Experience Highlights

A good value for money experience that mixes ancient Tokyo with modern Tokyo and appeals to both young and not so young alike.
